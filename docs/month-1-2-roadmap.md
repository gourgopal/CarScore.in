# Month 1-2 Roadmap

## Goal
Build the foundation for CarScore.in, focusing on robust calculators and an initial static catalogue, optimized for Cloudflare free-tier hosting.

## In Scope
1. Repository assessment and architecture documentation (Current)
2. Design-system foundation
3. Public homepage
4. Data provenance framework
5. Small verified current-car catalogue (10-15 manufacturers, 30-50 models)
6. Car search and selection
7. N-car Total Cost of Ownership comparison
8. Calculators: Fuel-cost, EV charging-cost, EMI, on-road price, ICE vs EV break-even
9. Public car pages (for approved records)
10. Calculator and methodology landing pages
11. SEO architecture & Google Search Console readiness
12. Privacy-conscious analytics
13. Google AdSense readiness (behind feature flag)
14. Cloudflare Pages deployment
15. Simple data-import and validation workflow
16. Tests, documentation, and deployment validation

## Out of Scope for Month 1-2
- Used-car marketplace or user-generated listings
- Live quotes (insurance, loans) or payment processing
- User authentication
- Scraping or massive auto-generated pages
- Mobile application
